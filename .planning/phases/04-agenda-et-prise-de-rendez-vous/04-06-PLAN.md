<objective>
Open the trainer's back-office: a sober, role-gated `/admin` shell, and the two screens that make the
availability model editable — weekly working hours with range exceptions, and the French public
holiday calendar.

Purpose: D-01 puts the back-office in its own route group so Lot 10 extends this shell rather than
re-cutting it, and this is the very first exposure of the `administrator` role, which has sat in the
database since Lot 3 with no surface at all. Until this plan lands, the seeded typical week is the
only availability anyone can define.

Output: `requireAdministrator()`, the `/admin` shell at compact density, the horaires and jours
fériés screens, and their write route handlers.
</objective>

The tables this plan edits, from plan 04-01, both governed by an admin-only RLS policy
(`dispo_admin_all`, `exception_admin_all`) evaluated through `app.est_administrateur()`:

```sql
app.disponibilite_hebdomadaire (id uuid, jour_semaine smallint /* ISO 1=lundi … 7=dimanche */,
                                heure_debut time, heure_fin time, actif boolean, …)
app.exception_agenda (id uuid, jour date, ouvert boolean default false,
                      motif text in ('blocage','ferie','ouverture'), libelle text,
                      heure_debut time, heure_fin time, …)
-- both null hours = whole day; a ferie row is seeded ouvert=false and is reopened by flipping ouvert
app.est_administrateur() returns boolean   -- security definer, used by the policies
```
